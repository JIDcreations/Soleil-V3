# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static multi-page site, Astro (static output) so pages share layout and data without a client framework; vanilla JS islands for motion. No backend.

## Users

Mostly women from Beervelde and the wider Gent/Lochristi area, 30 to 65, who want visibly better skin (acne, pigmentation, ageing, dull skin) or a moment of rest. They come from Instagram, Google or word of mouth, usually on a phone, and want to know: is this the right place, what does it cost, how do I book.

## Product Purpose

Website of Instituut Soleil, a one-woman skin institute run by its owner (name to confirm) at Rivierstraat 70, 9080 Beervelde. Success = a visitor books (Optios booking link) or starts with a skin analysis / Discovery.

## Positioning

Skin improvement with proven methods and honest, personal advice from one expert with 27 years of experience (per the Sept 2026 news post). No quick fixes: analysis first (Lightskin 3D scan), treatment on measure, and home care (75% of the result comes from what you do at home). Works with Environ, Exuviance, Jane Iredale.

## Operating Context

Booking via https://booking.optios.net/18875. Webshop at https://www.webshopsoleil.be/. The owner does not pick up the phone during treatments (leave name + number, she calls back). Duo cabin and relax room on site. Laser hair removal via partner Haarlaser Team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Content, prices, hours and treatments are carried over from Soleil-Hifi_V2/assets/js/data.js (confirmed by the user to reuse as-is). Still to confirm with the client: exact years in business, opening hours (two versions exist), VAT number, Neo-Lift and huidverbetering prices, Discovery duration, Duo Relax price. Photos are often low-res (357 px).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Instituut Soleil. Logo: a thin hand-drawn sun arc rising over two horizon strokes, in a warm golden tan (LOGO-Scrape screenshot, no vector delivered). The new logo is binding; the V2 look (mustard field, cacao brown, Zodiak serif, old script "Soleil" logo) was rejected by the client as ugly and not fresh. Voice: first person ("ik"), Dutch (nl-BE), warm, direct, no hype. Owner name unconfirmed (Cathy is the partner laser specialist from Haarlaser Team, not the owner).

## Evidence on Hand

Owner and treatment photos in Soleil-Hifi_V2/assets/img and _bron. No real reviews on hand: do not fabricate testimonials. No vector logo.

## Product Principles

- Analysis before treatment: the site always offers "start with a skin analysis" to newcomers.
- Honest over glossy: real prices, real people, no invented claims.
- Booking is never more than one tap away.
- It is one person's practice: her voice ("ik") and face carry the trust.
