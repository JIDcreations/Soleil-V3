# Surface brief: Soleil V3 site (all pages, home leads)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: women 30 to 65 around Beervelde/Gent wanting better skin or rest, mostly on phone. Action: book via Optios, or start with huidanalyse/Discovery.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, airy skin-expert site where the client's real face, real prices and real method carry trust. Refuses the category default of spa stock + pastel cards, and refuses V2's mustard field, cacao brown and sun-arch motifs everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white paper (#fbfaf8), deep warm night (#1c1917) for one offer band and the footer, sand (#f3eee8) for the method and arrangements bands. The logo tan (#bf8e63) only for the mark, Garamond numerals and prices; tan-ink (#8a5a30) for text and the primary pill. Albert Sans light at large sizes for headings, EB Garamond for the wordmark, the statement, step numerals and prices. Photos square-cornered; anything you press is a pill; hairline lists instead of cards.

STORY: Visitor sees who she is and what she does in one viewport, understands the 3-step method (analysis, treatment, home care), scans treatments with prices, is offered a clear start (Discovery €100), sees pampering and brands, and books. Booking is one tap away everywhere (header pill, mobile dock).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: the Soleil mark draws itself, then a three-line light headline "Stralen begint bij een huid in balans.", a 14-word lede, the tan "Afspraak maken" pill and the "Nieuw? Begin met een huidanalyse" link, and a foot rule with a live open/closed status, address and phone. Right 45%: the owner portrait with a smaller treatment photo overlapping its lower left. Header: mark + wordmark, single-line nav, ink booking pill.

FORM: Editorial skin-clinic site, own grounded direction chosen after the user rejected the concept roll (seed key 1dfe9325, roll not used). Signature interactions: logo draw-on + clip reveals on load; statement words ink in on scroll; method section with a sticky image that swaps per step; treatment index rows with a pointer-following photo; arrangements rail; live opening-hours status.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
